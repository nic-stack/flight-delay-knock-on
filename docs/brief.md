# Where airline delay starts, and what an airline controls

**About 45% of delay starts with the airline once knock-on delays are followed back, and morning delays do the most damage.** *The shares are measured; the actions are our judgment.*

## What we found

October 2025 to September 2026, 7.06M flights, 14 airlines (BTS on-time data). About 23% of completed flights arrived 15+ minutes late.

**Knock-on delay** is delay passed from one flight to the next on the same plane. Airlines file it as "late aircraft" (40% of delay minutes), which is accurate for that flight. Following it back to the flight where it started:

| Where delay starts | Reported for each flight on its own | With knock-on delay followed back |
|---|:-:|:-:|
| The airline | 33% | **45%** (likely ~52%, at most 59%) |
| Air traffic system | 21% | 33% |
| Weather | 6% | 8% |
| Late aircraft | 40% | shared out above |
| Start unknown | – | 15% |

This doesn't contradict the official figures. It answers a different question with the same data: not "what made this flight late?" but "where did the delay begin?"

**Morning delay spreads, evening delay stops.** A 15-minute delay on an 8am departure causes about 10 more minutes on the plane's later flights; at 9pm, under 1. Across the morning it is 9.0 minutes, against 2.5 in the evening (3.6 times as much). Evening flights pick up the most new delay, but planes stop overnight.

**The first flight of the day is the most fixable.** 56% of the knock-on delay it causes starts with the airline, against 45% for later flights.

## What to do

1. **Protect the first flight of the day:** a daily readiness target for crew, maintenance and boarding.
2. **Buffer mornings, not evenings:** add turnaround time to morning rotations first.
3. **Track delay where it starts:** report knock-on delay by its starting cause each month, with the unknown share shown.

## How sure

- **Checked:** our monthly on-time rate is within 0.35 points of BTS's published figures, and every delay minute is accounted for after following knock-on delay back.
- **Limits:** causes are self-reported by airlines; delays under 15 minutes have no recorded cause, so 15% of minutes can't be followed back; splitting knock-on delay in proportion to the earlier flight's causes is an assumption.
